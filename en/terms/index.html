You decide what gets deleted

Wayout deletes nothing on its own. It shows the list of what you are about to free up and asks you to confirm. Anything that comes back, such as caches or node_modules, is deleted permanently and cannot be restored from the Recycle Bin. Anything the app marks "handle with care" goes to the Windows Recycle Bin if it is 1 GB or less. In Settings you can send everything up to 1 GB to the Recycle Bin. Every deletion is recorded in the clean-up log.

The caches and folders it offers are recreated when you use your tools, though that can take time and downloads. Wayout shows on each row how it comes back. Review the list before you confirm.

Actions that need care

  • Cleaning stopped Docker containers and volumes can remove data stored in them, such as databases. Wayout warns you first.
  • Ending a process closes that program and anything unsaved in it is lost.
